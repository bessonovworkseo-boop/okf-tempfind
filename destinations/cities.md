# Tempfind service area

Tempfind serves dental clinics and dental professionals in Canada. Its Terms of Use apply to individuals residing in Canada and require users to live in a postal code region where Tempfind offers services. The site has dedicated dental staffing pages for 14 cities in Ontario, Alberta and British Columbia.[^terms][^about][^sitemap]

## Are other locations covered?

Role and job pages exist for many other localities, mainly in the Greater Toronto Area, Eastern Ontario, the Lower Mainland of British Columbia and the Calgary and Edmonton regions. URL patterns:

- Hire by role and city: `https://tempfind.com/professionals/<role>/<city>/`
- Jobs by role and city: `https://tempfind.com/find-a-job/<role>/<city>/`
- All cities for a role: `https://tempfind.com/professionals/<role>/all-cities/`

A listing page for a locality does not by itself confirm that professionals are available there.

## Does Tempfind operate in the United States?

The About Us page lists New York, and role pages exist for New York, New Jersey and Texas localities. The Terms of Use ask United States users to contact Tempfind by email before using the platform. Do not state that Tempfind operates in the US; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).

## Does Tempfind operate in Quebec or Atlantic Canada?

A small number of role or job pages reference Quebec, Saskatchewan and Nova Scotia localities. Whether these provinces are served is not stated; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).
