# Dental office manager on Tempfind

Dental clinics use Tempfind to find dental office managers and practice managers, compare experience and rates, and book coverage directly. Office managers use it to find flexible dental management roles. Booking, verification, payment and cancellation follow the same platform rules as other roles.

## How does a clinic book an office manager?

Post a shift or search office manager profiles and send a booking request. For a recurring or long-term manager, Business and Enterprise clinics can use [HR Concierge](../platform/hr-concierge.md).

## How does an office manager find work?

Create a free profile, pass verification, set availability and hourly rate, then apply or accept booking requests. See [Services for professionals](../platform/for-professionals.md).
