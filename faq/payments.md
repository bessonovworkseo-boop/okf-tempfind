# Tempfind payments FAQ

## How do clinics pay for shifts?
By credit card through Tempfind, processed by Stripe. Under the current Payment Policy, all clinics use Tempfind card processing from January 1, 2026. Tempfind does not store card details.[^payment]

## Can a clinic pay a professional directly by e-transfer or cheque?
No. The HR Concierge page states that e-transfer and cheque payments to professionals are not allowed, and the Payment Policy routes all clinic payments through Tempfind. Some older Help Center articles still describe direct payment; the policy takes precedence.[^hr][^payment]

## When do professionals get paid?
The professional submits hours after the shift. The clinic has 24 hours to confirm or dispute them, otherwise they are auto-approved. Payment is disbursed within 3 to 5 business days after approval.[^payment]

## What if a professional is sent home early?
If it is not the professional's fault, they are paid for the scheduled hours.[^payment]

## How are billing disputes handled?
Clinics report billing disputes within 10 business days of the charge date, and Tempfind aims to respond within 10 business days. Unauthorized chargebacks may lead to suspension.[^payment]
