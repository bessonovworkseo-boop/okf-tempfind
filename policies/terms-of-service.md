# Tempfind Terms of Use summary

The Tempfind Terms of Use govern use of the platform by dental clinics and dental professionals. The current text is Version 4, updated August 11, 2026; the original effective date is September 25, 2024. This file is a summary; the full Terms on tempfind.com prevail.[^terms][^privacy]

## Who operates the platform?

Tempfind Inc., a corporation incorporated in Ontario, with the address 20 Bay Street, Suite 1100, Toronto, ON M5J 2N8 stated in the Terms.

## Who can use Tempfind?

- Individuals residing in Canada. Users in the United States are asked to contact Tempfind by email before using the platform.
- Users at least 18 years old who can form binding contracts.
- Users residing in a postal code region where Tempfind offers services.
- Professionals who are Canadian citizens, legal residents or hold a valid Canadian visa permitting work.
- Users in good standing who hold any required licensing or certification.
- Users who represent they have no relevant criminal record and are not registered sex offenders.

## What is Tempfind's role?

- Tempfind is not an employer of professionals and not an employment or recruitment agency for any professional.
- Tempfind is not a party to any agreement between a clinic and a professional.
- Clinics are the potential temporary employers and must comply with employment, safety and workers' compensation laws.

## Which law applies?

The laws of the Province of Ontario, with exclusive jurisdiction of Ontario courts, subject to the consumer protection laws of the user's province or territory.

## How are changes made?

Significant changes take effect at least 30 days after notice. Non-material changes take effect when posted.
