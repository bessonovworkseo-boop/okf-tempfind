# Tempfind dental staffing glossary

Definitions of terms used on tempfind.com. General industry definitions are kept neutral; platform-specific terms describe how Tempfind uses them. Professional scope of practice is set by provincial regulators and differs by province.[^clinics][^pricing][^nc]

## Roles

**RDH (registered dental hygienist).** A dental hygienist registered with the provincial dental hygiene regulator. See [RDH on Tempfind](registered-dental-hygienist.md).

**Dental assistant, Level I and Level II.** Ontario uses levels to describe dental assistant training and permitted duties; Level II assistants can perform more intra-oral tasks. Other provinces use different titles. Tempfind lets clinics filter by level. See [Dental assistant](dental-assistant.md).

**RDA.** Registered dental assistant, a title used in some provinces for a dental assistant registered with the provincial body.

**Dentist (DDS).** Doctor of Dental Surgery. Tempfind uses this label for locum and associate dentists. See [Dentist (DDS)](dentist-dds.md).

**Locum dentist.** A dentist who temporarily covers for another dentist or fills a short-term gap.

**Associate dentist.** A dentist working in a practice they do not own.

**Dental floater.** A cross-trained team member who covers more than one role in a clinic. See [Dental floater](dental-floater.md).

**Sedation or general nurse (RN).** A registered nurse who supports sedation or general nursing tasks in a dental clinic. See [Sedation nurse](sedation-general-nurse-rn.md).

**HARP.** In Ontario, a certification associated with the Healing Arts Radiation Protection Act that relates to operating dental X-ray equipment. Tempfind profiles may list it.

## Business terms

**DSO.** Dental service organization; a group that runs or supports multiple dental practices. Tempfind's Enterprise plan targets multi-location practices and DSOs.

**Temp shift.** A single, short-term period of work, typically 6 to 8 hours on Tempfind.

**Ongoing assignment and multi-day assignment.** Tempfind job types for repeated or consecutive shifts with the same clinic.

## Tempfind platform terms

**Booking request (direct booking request).** A request from a clinic to a specific professional for a shift. The first to accept gets it.

**Job posting.** A shift published by a clinic on the job board for professionals to apply to.

**Favourites and automatic acceptance.** Saved professionals a clinic prefers; Help Center describes automatic acceptance for favourites.

**Placement fee (Tempfinder fee).** The $49 fee per completed shift on the Pay Per Shift plan. See [Clinic plans and fees](../products/pricing.md).

**Payment handling fee.** 3.9% of each shift total, charged to clinics. See [Payments and payouts](../policies/payment-policy.md).

**HR Concierge.** Tempfind's assisted matching service for recurring and long-term roles. See [HR Concierge](../products/hr-concierge.md).

**Accountability deposit.** A $500 deposit Tempfind may require from a professional after repeated cancellations. See [Cancellation rules](../policies/cancellation-policy.md).

**Circumvention.** Arranging work or payment off-platform with a Tempfind-connected party. See [Non-circumvention rules](../policies/non-circumvention-policy.md).
