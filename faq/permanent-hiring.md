# Permanent and long-term hiring on Tempfind FAQ

## Can a clinic hire a Tempfind professional permanently?
Yes, through HR Concierge and the Direct Recruitment Policy, which carries a one-time agency fee based on role and experience. The amount is not published on the pages reviewed.[^hr][^nc]

## What is HR Concierge?
An assisted matching service for recurring weekly shifts, short-term contracts and direct hires. Tempfind interviews and shortlists candidates, the clinic interviews them and runs a trial shift. It is included in the Business and Enterprise plans. See [HR Concierge](../products/hr-concierge.md).[^hr]

## What if Tempfind finds no candidates?
If Tempfind cannot present viable candidates within 30 days, it cancels the membership and refunds one month, unless the clinic booked other temps through the platform in that period.[^hr]

## Can a clinic hire a temp it met on Tempfind outside the platform?
Not within 12 months of the last Tempfind-facilitated interaction. Off-platform arrangements breach the Non-Circumvention Policy; a confirmed attempt by a clinic carries a $5,000 fee. Relationships that existed before Tempfind are excluded.[^nc]

## Do role pages say the platform is temporary only?
Yes. Self-serve booking is for temporary coverage; long-term and permanent hiring goes through HR Concierge.
