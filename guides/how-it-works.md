# How Tempfind works

Tempfind is a two-sided marketplace. Dental clinics publish shifts or book professionals directly, and dental professionals apply to shifts or accept booking requests. Both sides communicate through built-in chat, and shift payments go through the platform.[^home][^clinics][^professionals][^jobs]

## How does a clinic book a temporary dental professional?

Clinics have two booking routes.

**Route 1. Post a shift (job board)**

1. Register the clinic and complete the clinic profile.
2. Create a shift posting with role, date, start time, duration, qualifications and duties.
3. Review applicants. Profiles show education, work history, certifications, languages, hourly rate and ratings from past shifts.
4. Accept an applicant to confirm the shift.

**Route 2. Search and request**

1. Search the professional pool by role and location and filter by availability.
2. Send a booking request to one or several professionals. The first professional to accept gets the shift.
3. Optionally add professionals to Favourites; Help Center describes automatic acceptance for favourite professionals.

After the shift, the professional submits worked hours, the clinic approves or disputes them, and payment is processed. See [Payments and payouts](../policies/payment-policy.md).

Typical shift length shown on role pages is 6 to 8 hours. Shorter and longer bookings are possible.

## How does a dental professional find shifts?

1. Create a free profile with education, experience, certifications, languages and availability.
2. Upload required documents and pass verification. Until verified, a professional cannot apply to jobs or receive bookings. See [Professional verification](../concepts/professional-verification.md).
3. Set an hourly rate and open availability in the calendar.
4. Apply to posted shifts using filters for profession, location, dates, job type and hourly rate, or accept booking requests from clinics that find the profile in search.
5. Work the shift, submit hours, and receive payment. See [Payments and payouts](../policies/payment-policy.md).

Listing types on the job board include single-day shifts, ongoing coverage and multi-day assignments.

## What about recurring or long-term staffing?

Clinics on the Business or Enterprise plan can use [HR Concierge](../products/hr-concierge.md): Tempfind interviews and shortlists candidates, the clinic interviews and runs a trial shift, then books recurring shifts or proceeds to a direct hire under the Direct Recruitment Policy.

Role landing pages state that the self-serve platform covers temporary work only. Permanent hiring goes through HR Concierge and the Direct Recruitment Policy. A Help Center article also mentions permanent postings; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).

## What does Tempfind not do?

- It does not employ professionals and is not a party to the clinic–professional agreement.
- It does not handle payroll or benefits for clinics.
- Clinics remain responsible for employment, safety and workers' compensation compliance.
