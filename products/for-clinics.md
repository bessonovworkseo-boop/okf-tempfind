# Tempfind for dental clinics

Tempfind lets dental clinics in Canada fill temporary shifts with verified dental professionals. Clinics post shifts or book professionals directly, chat with them before the shift, and pay through the platform. Multi-location practices and DSOs can use the Enterprise plan.[^clinics][^pricing][^help-clinics]

## Which roles can a clinic book?

Registered dental hygienists, dental assistants, dental receptionists, dentists (DDS, including locum and associate dentists), dental office managers and dental floaters. Sedation or general nurses (RN) have role pages; the clinics page marks this role as coming soon in one section. See [Roles](../concepts/index.md).

## What booking options are available?

| Option | How it works |
|---|---|
| Post and hire | Publish a shift, review applicants, accept one |
| Search and request | Browse professionals and send booking requests; first to accept gets the shift |
| Bulk shift invitations | Invite several professionals to a shift at once (Help Center) |
| Favourites | Save professionals and use automatic acceptance for favourites (Help Center) |
| HR Concierge | Recurring weekly shifts, short-term contracts and direct hire support (Business and Enterprise plans) |

Clinics can also post multi-day and permanent postings according to the Help Center article "Can I post permanent or multi day job postings?" Role pages say the self-serve platform is for temporary work only; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).

## What tools does a clinic account include?

- Clinic profile and preferences
- Shift posting, editing and cancellation
- Applicant review with profiles, resumes, ratings and completed-shift counts
- Chat with professionals before booking
- Hours approval and dispute within 24 hours of submission
- Payment history and invoices in the Billing section
- Ratings and reviews of professionals after each shift
- Notification settings

## How are professionals checked?

Tempfind staff manually review each professional's documents after registration. A professional cannot apply to jobs or receive bookings until verified. See [Professional verification](../concepts/professional-verification.md).

## How much does it cost?

Clinics choose Pay Per Shift ($49 per completed shift), Business ($149 per month per location, 12-month term) or Enterprise (custom). A 3.9% payment handling fee applies to each shift total. See [Clinic plans and fees](pricing.md).

## What is the clinic responsible for?

- Setting shift details and approving submitted hours.
- Paying for shifts through Tempfind.
- Complying with employment, safety and workers' compensation laws.
- Booking and paying through the platform for 12 months after the last Tempfind-facilitated interaction. See [Non-circumvention rules](../policies/non-circumvention-policy.md).
