# Tempfind shift cancellation rules

Tempfind has separate cancellation policies for professionals and for clinics. Professionals must cancel confirmed shifts on the platform at least 48 hours before the start time. Short-notice cancellations and no-shows lead to immediate deactivation of booking features.[^cancellation][^cancellation-pros][^cancellation-clinics][^help-cancel]

## What are the rules for professionals?

Source: Shift Cancellation Policy For Professionals, Version 2.0.

- A standard cancellation needs at least 48 hours' notice before the shift start.
- Cancellations must be submitted on the platform with a valid reason. Cancelling by phone, text or email counts as a no-show.
- The first three standard cancellations are recorded on the profile and may lower search visibility. A fourth may trigger a review and an accountability deposit.
- A cancellation with less than 48 hours' notice deactivates booking and application features immediately. Upcoming confirmed shifts are cancelled and reassigned.
- A no-show leads to immediate account deactivation and reassignment of upcoming shifts.
- Emergency documentation (medical note, government emergency document or equivalent) must be submitted within two calendar days.

## What is the accountability deposit?

At Tempfind's discretion, a professional may need to pay a $500 deposit to restore booking features. It is refunded after 10 completed shifts with no further cancellations or no-shows. A later cancellation or missed shift leads to permanent removal, and the deposit goes to the affected clinics.

## Can decisions be appealed?

Temporary deactivations can be appealed by email. Permanent removal cannot be appealed.

## What are the rules for clinics?

- Clinics cancel a confirmed shift in the Inbox: open the shift and select Cancel Shift.
- The clinic policy covers notice periods, refunds and cancellation fees for confirmed shifts.
- If a clinic cancels less than 24 hours before a shift, the professional may be eligible for cancellation compensation if they make good-faith efforts to find a comparable replacement shift.

Notice periods and compensation amounts for clinic cancellations are in the Shift Cancellation Policy for Clinics, which this record does not summarize; check https://tempfind.com/cancellation-policy-for-clinics/ before answering, and see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).

## What happens if a professional does not arrive?

Help Center articles "What happens if the Professional did not show up for their shift?" and "What Should I Do When a Professional Didn't Arrive to Their Shift" describe the clinic-side steps; this record does not summarize them.
