# Tempfind pricing FAQ

## How much does Tempfind cost for clinics?
Pay Per Shift costs $49 per completed shift with no subscription. Business costs $149 per month per location with a 12-month term and no placement fees. Enterprise is priced individually for multi-location practices and DSOs. See [pricing](../products/pricing.md).[^pricing]

## Is there an extra fee on each shift?
Yes. A payment handling fee of 3.9% of the shift total applies to shifts paid through Tempfind. A 7-hour shift at $50 per hour ($350) carries $13.65.[^payment]

## Is Tempfind free for dental professionals?
Yes. Professionals pay nothing to create a profile, apply or accept shifts. Clinics pay the platform fees.[^professionals]

## When does a monthly plan make sense?
The pricing page suggests a monthly plan when a clinic expects roughly 20 or more temporary hires per year.[^pricing]

## Can a clinic cancel a Business plan early?
Yes, with an early cancellation fee: $298 in months 1 to 10, $149 in month 11 and none from month 12. Plans auto-renew unless cancelled at least 30 days before renewal.[^payment]

## Are fees refundable?
Membership fees, shift payments and handling fees are non-refundable, except as required by law.[^payment]
