# Professional verification on Tempfind

Every dental professional on Tempfind goes through verification right after registration. Tempfind employees review the submitted documents manually, and the system makes no automatic decisions. Until a professional receives verified status, they cannot apply to jobs or be booked.

## What does Tempfind check?

According to the Help Center:

- Professional credentials, certifications and education, supported by documents.
- Personal identification and security checks, where required for the profession.
- Authenticity and validity of submitted documents.
- Skills, clinical experience and work history provided by the professional.
- Confirmation with educational institutions and regulatory or other organizations, when required.

According to role pages:

- A valid licence number is required before a hygienist profile goes live.
- Verified badges cover government ID, professional licence, police check (when applicable) and certification documents.

The clinics page also mentions one-on-one onboarding covering reliability, patient care and code of conduct. [NEEDS CONFIRMATION: whether every professional has an interview or only HR Concierge candidates.]

## Who performs verification?

Tempfind staff. The Help Center states the process is fully controlled by employees.

## What do clinics see after verification?

- Verified badges on the profile.
- Education, work history, software experience, languages and certifications.
- Star ratings and number of completed shifts.
- Hourly rate.

Clinics rate professionals after each completed shift. Professionals can also rate clinics. Clinics submit practice credentials during registration.

## What is the clinic still responsible for?

Clinics remain responsible for their own legal compliance as the party engaging the professional. Tempfind's Terms of Use require users to hold any licensing or certification required for their profession.
