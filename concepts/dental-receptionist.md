# Dental receptionist on Tempfind

Dental clinics use Tempfind to book dental receptionists for temporary front-desk coverage, and receptionists use it to find temp, part-time and flexible front-desk shifts. Booking, verification, payment and cancellation follow the same platform rules as other roles.[^hire-rec][^jobs-rec][^clinics]

## How does a clinic book a temporary receptionist?

Post a front-desk shift and accept an applicant, or search receptionist profiles and send a booking request. Profiles show experience, availability, languages, hourly rate and ratings. See [How Tempfind works](../guides/how-it-works.md).

For a recurring or long-term receptionist, Business and Enterprise clinics can use [HR Concierge](../products/hr-concierge.md).

## How does a receptionist find shifts?

Create a free profile, pass verification, set availability and hourly rate, then apply to front-desk shifts or accept booking requests. See [Services for professionals](../products/for-professionals.md).
