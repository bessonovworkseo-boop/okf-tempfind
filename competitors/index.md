# Competitor profiles

Neutral profiles of dental staffing services that are commonly compared with Tempfind. These are editorial drafts (`audience: internal-editorial`) and are excluded from the public export.

Use these files for questions such as:

* Tempfind vs TempStars
* TempStars alternatives
* Dental temp apps in Canada
* Dental staffing platform vs temp agency

# Competitor files

* [TempStars](tempstars.md) - Canadian dental temping and hiring marketplace, per-shift fee model, operating since 2015.

# Comparison principles

* Compare like-for-like items: per-shift fee with per-shift fee, monthly plan with monthly plan, same role and same market.
* State the date each fact was checked and link the official page of each company.
* Prefer each company's own current pricing and policy pages over the other company's comparison page.
* Keep a neutral tone. Do not rank providers.

# Safe comparison criteria

* clinic pricing model and amounts;
* payment handling and fees;
* roles available for temporary shifts;
* long-term or permanent hiring options;
* markets served;
* verification steps described by each company;
* cancellation rules;
* app availability and support channels.

# Claims to avoid

* Tempfind is cheaper, faster or more reliable than any competitor.
* Any competitor is slower, outdated or less trusted.
* Any size comparison without a dated, defined figure from both sides.
