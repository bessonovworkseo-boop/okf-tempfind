# Tempfind in Ottawa

Dental clinics in Ottawa, Ontario use Tempfind to book temporary dental professionals and to hire permanent staff through HR Concierge. Professionals in Ottawa use it to find temp shifts. Posting a shift notifies local professionals, who can accept it.[^ottawa][^ottawa-all]

## Which roles can Ottawa clinics book?

Registered dental hygienists, dental assistants, associate or locum dentists (DDS), receptionists, sedation or general nurses (RN), dental office or practice managers and dental floaters. See [Roles](../concepts/index.md).

## How does an Ottawa clinic book a temp?

1. Post a shift.
2. Review matches.
3. Check profiles and credentials.
4. Confirm the booking.
5. Complete the shift and pay online.

Clinics can also search by role, filter by availability and send booking requests. There is no minimum number of shifts.

## Can an Ottawa clinic hire permanent staff?

Yes, through [HR Concierge](../products/hr-concierge.md): share role details, take an intake call, receive a shortlist, run a trial shift, then hire directly or book recurring shifts.

## How does payment work?

Clinics pay through the platform after the shift. Invoices are generated automatically and professionals are paid out. See [Payments and payouts](../policies/payment-policy.md) and [Clinic plans and fees](../products/pricing.md).

## Is service available in French?

The Ottawa page is in English only and does not mention French-language service; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).
