# Tempfind coverage FAQ

## Where does Tempfind operate?
In Canada. The Terms of Use apply to individuals residing in Canada and require users to live in a postal code region where Tempfind offers services.[^terms]

## Which cities have a dedicated Tempfind page?
Toronto, Ottawa and Kingston in Ontario; Calgary, Edmonton, Red Deer, Lethbridge and Airdrie in Alberta; Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby, Richmond, Victoria and Kelowna in British Columbia. See [cities](../destinations/cities.md).[^about]

## Does Tempfind work in the United States?
The Terms ask US users to contact Tempfind by email before using the platform. The site lists New York and has role pages for some US localities. Current US availability is unconfirmed; do not state that Tempfind operates in the US.[^terms][^about]

## Does a role page for a city mean professionals are available there?
No. A listing page shows that the URL exists, not how many professionals are active in that city.
