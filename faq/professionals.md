# Working on Tempfind FAQ for dental professionals

## Who can register as a professional?
People aged 18 or older who are Canadian citizens, legal residents or hold a valid Canadian visa permitting work, who hold any licensing or certification their profession requires, and who live in a postal code region Tempfind serves.[^terms]

## Does it cost anything to join?
No. Creating a profile, applying and accepting shifts is free for professionals.[^professionals]

## Who sets my hourly rate?
You do, in your profile. Clinics see it in search and on your applications. Each job posting also lists its offered pay.[^professionals][^jobs]

## How do I find shifts?
Filter the job board by profession, location, dates, job type and hourly rate and apply, or wait for booking requests from clinics that find your profile. Listing types include single-day shifts, ongoing coverage and multi-day assignments.[^jobs]

## Am I an employee of Tempfind?
No. The Terms state that Tempfind is not an employer of professionals and not a party to the agreement between a clinic and a professional.[^terms]
