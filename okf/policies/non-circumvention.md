# Tempfind non-circumvention rules

Clinics and professionals connected through Tempfind must book and pay temp work through the platform for 12 months after their most recent Tempfind-facilitated interaction. Arranging the same work off-platform is circumvention and carries fees. The policy is Version 1.0, effective August 11, 2026.

## What counts as circumvention?

- Arranging temp work, recurring coverage, direct payment, employment, contracting or other paid work with a Tempfind-connected party outside the platform, unless Tempfind approves it in writing.
- Doing the same through a third party, another location or an affiliate.
- Proposing or agreeing to an off-platform arrangement, even if no shift, payment or hire follows.

Both parties are responsible, even if the other side proposed it.

## What is excluded?

Relationships that existed independently before Tempfind involvement. A prior acquaintance alone does not qualify.

## What are the fees?

| Case | Fee |
|---|---|
| Clinic, confirmed attempt with no off-platform engagement | $5,000 per attempt |
| Professional, confirmed occurrence | $1,000 per occurrence, plus possible suspension or termination |
| Completed off-platform work | Applicable circumvention fee plus related booking, service or payment fees |
| Direct hire | Agency fee under the Direct Recruitment Policy [NEEDS CONFIRMATION: amount] |

Confirmation requires reasonable evidence such as written communications or a substantiated report.

## How can a clinic and professional keep working together legitimately?

- Temporary shifts: Direct Booking Request or Favourites.
- Recurring coverage: Ongoing Assignment Request or [HR Concierge](../platform/hr-concierge.md).
- Permanent hiring: Direct Recruitment process before finalizing, subject to eligibility and the agency fee.

## Can a decision be appealed?

Yes, by written appeal within 7 days of notice. An appeal does not lift restrictions automatically.
