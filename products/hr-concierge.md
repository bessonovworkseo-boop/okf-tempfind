# Tempfind HR Concierge

HR Concierge is an assisted hiring service for dental clinics that need recurring weekly shifts, short-term contracts or a direct hire. Tempfind interviews and shortlists pre-screened candidates, the clinic interviews them and runs a trial shift, then books recurring shifts through the Tempfind app. It is included in the Business and Enterprise plans and in the grandfathered Essential plan, not in Pay Per Shift.[^hr][^hr-policy][^ottawa]

## How does HR Concierge work?

1. The clinic submits job and candidate requirements.
2. Tempfind interviews and shortlists candidates.
3. The clinic interviews selected candidates (the page mentions a 15-minute interview).
4. The clinic books a candidate for a trial shift.
5. The clinic books recurring shifts in the Tempfind app. Tempfind handles scheduling and payment.

If a trial candidate is not suitable, Tempfind moves on to the next pre-screened candidate.

## Which roles does it cover?

Registered dental hygienists, dental assistants, receptionists, dentists (DDS), sedation or general nurses (RN), dental office managers and dental floaters.

## What does it cost?

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Business plan | $149 per month per location, unlimited monthly shifts |
| Enterprise plan | Custom pricing |
| Payment handling fee | 3.9% per shift |
| Direct hire | One-time agency fee based on role and experience; amount not published |
| Off-platform deal without notifying Tempfind | $2,750 agency finder fee (HR Concierge page) |

## What happens if no candidates are found?

If Tempfind cannot present viable candidates within 30 days, it cancels the membership and refunds one month of service. The refund does not apply if the clinic books other temps through the platform during that period.

## Can a clinic hire an HR Concierge candidate directly?

Yes, through the Direct Recruitment Policy and its agency fee. Tempfind recommends working together for 2 to 3 months first. If a direct hire leaves within three months, Tempfind states it replaces them at no extra cost.

## What is the employment relationship?

Professionals are independent contractors according to the HR Concierge page. Clinics are responsible for their own compliance with employment and labour laws. Candidate profiles, compensation and clinic requirements are kept confidential among Tempfind, the clinic and the professional.
