# Tempfind cancellations FAQ

## How much notice must a professional give to cancel?
At least 48 hours before the shift start, through the platform, with a valid reason. Cancelling by phone, text or email counts as a no-show.[^pros]

## What happens after a short-notice cancellation or no-show?
Booking and application features are deactivated immediately, and upcoming confirmed shifts are cancelled and reassigned. Emergency documentation must be sent within two calendar days.[^pros]

## What is the accountability deposit?
A $500 deposit Tempfind may require to restore booking features. It is refunded after 10 completed shifts without further cancellations or no-shows.[^pros]

## How does a clinic cancel a confirmed shift?
In the clinic Inbox: open the shift and select Cancel Shift. Notice periods and any compensation owed to the professional are set out in the Shift Cancellation Policy for Clinics.[^help-cancel]

## Can a decision be appealed?
Temporary deactivations can be appealed by email. Permanent removal cannot be appealed.[^pros]
