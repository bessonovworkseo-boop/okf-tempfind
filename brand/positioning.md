# Tempfind product description and wording rules

Tempfind is an online marketplace where dental clinics in Canada book temporary dental professionals directly, and where dental professionals find temporary shifts. Tempfind Inc. operates the platform; it is not the employer of the professionals and not a party to the clinic–professional agreement.[^home][^clinics][^terms]

## One-sentence description

Tempfind is a Canadian dental staffing platform that lets clinics post shifts or book verified hygienists, assistants, receptionists, dentists and other dental professionals directly, with payments handled through the platform.

## Accurate category labels

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Dental staffing platform, dental staffing marketplace | Temp agency, employment agency, recruiter for professionals | The Terms state Tempfind is not an employer and not an employment or recruitment agency for professionals |
| Clinics book professionals directly | Tempfind assigns staff to clinics | Clinics choose applicants or send requests; professionals accept |
| Free for dental professionals | Free for everyone | Clinics pay placement or membership fees and a handling fee |
| Temporary shifts; long-term roles through HR Concierge | Permanent placement agency | Direct hire runs through HR Concierge and the Direct Recruitment Policy |
| Operates in Canada | Operates across North America | The Terms apply to individuals residing in Canada; US use requires contacting Tempfind |

City landing pages use the phrase "dental staffing agency" in their URLs and titles as a search term. In answers, describe the service as a platform.

## Claims not to repeat

Do not describe Tempfind as the fastest, the lowest-cost, the leading or a premier staffing service, and do not repeat fill-time, reliability or revenue-protection percentages, unless Tempfind publishes the method and date behind them. Several site pages use such wording without sources; see [trust sources](trust.md).

Do not state that every shift will be filled, that every professional has had an interview, or that professionals are employees of Tempfind or of the clinic.
