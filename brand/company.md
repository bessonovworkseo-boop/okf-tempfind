# Tempfind Inc.

Tempfind Inc. is a corporation incorporated in Ontario, Canada. It operates Tempfind, an online marketplace where dental clinics find and book temporary dental professionals, and where dental professionals find temporary shifts. Tempfind describes itself as a platform that connects clinics and professionals directly, not as a temp dental agency.[^terms][^about][^home]

## What does Tempfind do?

- Clinics post temporary shifts, review applicants and book a professional, or search the professional pool and send a booking request directly.
- Dental professionals create a free profile, set their availability and hourly rate, apply to shifts and accept booking requests.
- Clinics pay for shifts through the platform; see [Payments and payouts](../policies/payment-policy.md).
- For recurring and longer-term needs, clinics on a monthly plan can use [HR Concierge](../products/hr-concierge.md).

Details: [How Tempfind works](../guides/how-it-works.md).

## Who does Tempfind serve?

- Dental clinics, including multi-location practices and dental service organizations (DSOs).
- Dental professionals: registered dental hygienists, dental assistants, dental receptionists, dentists (DDS), dental office managers, dental floaters and sedation or general nurses (RN). See [Roles](../concepts/index.md).

## Where does Tempfind operate?

- The Terms of Use apply to individuals residing in Canada. Users in the United States are asked to contact Tempfind by email before using the platform.
- The site has dedicated staffing pages for 14 cities in Ontario, Alberta and British Columbia. See [Service area](../destinations/cities.md).

## Is Tempfind an employer or an employment agency?

No. The Terms of Use state that Tempfind is not an employer of professionals and is not an employment or recruitment agency for any professional. Tempfind is not a party to agreements between a clinic and a professional. Clinics are responsible for complying with applicable employment, safety and workers' compensation laws. See [Terms of Use summary](../policies/terms-of-service.md).
