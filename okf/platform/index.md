# Platform and services

* [Tempfind for dental clinics](./for-clinics.md) - What dental clinics get from Tempfind - temporary shift booking, direct requests, chat, platform payments and HR Concierge for recurring roles.
* [Tempfind for dental professionals](./for-professionals.md) - How dental professionals join Tempfind for free, set their own rates and availability, apply to shifts and get paid after approved hours.
* [How Tempfind works](./how-it-works.md) - Step-by-step process for dental clinics booking temporary staff and for dental professionals finding shifts on Tempfind.
* [Tempfind HR Concierge](./hr-concierge.md) - HR Concierge is Tempfind's assisted matching service for recurring shifts, short-term contracts and direct hires, included in Business and Enterprise plans.
* [Payments and payouts on Tempfind](./payments.md) - How shift payments work on Tempfind - card payments through Stripe, hours approval within 24 hours, payout in 3 to 5 business days, 3.9% handling fee.
* [Professional verification on Tempfind](./verification.md) - How Tempfind staff manually verify dental professionals' credentials, documents and identity before they can apply to shifts or receive bookings.
