# About Tempfind FAQ

## What is Tempfind?
An online platform, operated by Tempfind Inc. in Ontario, that connects dental clinics in Canada with temporary dental professionals. Clinics post shifts or book professionals directly; professionals apply to shifts or accept booking requests.[^home][^terms]

## Is Tempfind a temp dental agency?
No. Tempfind describes itself as a marketplace where clinics and professionals connect directly. See [platform vs agency](../concepts/staffing-platform-vs-agency.md).[^home]

## Is Tempfind the employer of the professionals?
No. The Terms state that Tempfind is not an employer of professionals, not an employment or recruitment agency for any professional, and not a party to the clinic–professional agreement. Clinics are responsible for employment, safety and workers' compensation compliance.[^terms]

## Who founded Tempfind?
The About Us page lists Igor Shekaroff, Co-Founder and CEO, and Aleksandra Kovtanuka, Co-Founder and Director of Recruitment.[^about]

## Who is Tempfind for?
Dental clinics, including multi-location practices and DSOs, and dental professionals: hygienists, assistants, receptionists, dentists, office managers, floaters and nurses.
