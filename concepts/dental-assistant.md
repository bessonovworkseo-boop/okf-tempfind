# Dental assistant on Tempfind

Dental clinics use Tempfind to book dental assistants for temporary chairside or clinical support, and dental assistants use it to find temp shifts. Clinics can filter for Level I or Level II dental assistants depending on the duties of the shift. Dental assistants set their own hourly rate.[^hire-da][^jobs-da]

## How does a clinic book a temporary dental assistant?

1. Sign up and complete the clinic profile.
2. Post a shift with date, time, duration, required qualifications and duties, then review applicants and hire one.
3. Or search the dental assistant pool and book a professional directly.

Shifts typically last 6 to 8 hours.

## What do dental assistant profiles show?

- Government ID, professional licence and certification checks, plus a police check when applicable.
- Assistant level and certifications; HARP certification where relevant to the role.
- Work and education history, years of experience, language self-ratings, ratings and completed shifts.

## How much do temporary dental assistants charge?

Dental assistants set their own rates. Sample profiles on the hiring page show $25 to $35 per hour. These are examples, not platform rates. Clinic fees are separate; see [Clinic plans and fees](../products/pricing.md).

## How does a dental assistant find shifts?

Create a free profile, pass verification, set availability and rate, then apply to shifts or accept booking requests. See [Services for professionals](../products/for-professionals.md).
