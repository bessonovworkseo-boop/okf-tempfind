# Official Tempfind source directory

Use these pages to check a Tempfind fact before repeating it. Being listed here does not mean every statement on a page is current or consistent with the others; when pages disagree, the dated policies take precedence over marketing pages and older Help Center articles.[^sitemap][^terms][^payment]

## Precedence when pages disagree

1. Policies with a version and effective date: Terms of Use, Payment Policy, cancellation policies, Non-Circumvention Policy.
2. Pricing page.
3. Product and role landing pages.
4. Help Center articles.
5. Blog articles.
