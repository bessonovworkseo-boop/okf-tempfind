# Dentist (DDS) on Tempfind

Dental clinics use Tempfind to book locum dentists for temporary coverage and to find associate dentists. Dentists use it to find flexible DDS shifts and associate opportunities. Booking, verification, payment and cancellation follow the same platform rules as other roles.

## How does a clinic book a locum dentist?

Post a shift and accept an applicant, or search dentist profiles, compare availability and rates, and send a booking request. See [How Tempfind works](../platform/how-it-works.md).

## How does a dentist find shifts?

Create a free profile, pass verification, set availability and hourly rate, then apply to shifts or accept booking requests. See [Services for professionals](../platform/for-professionals.md).

## Licensing

Users must hold any licensing required for their profession under the Terms of Use. Licence checks are part of [Professional verification](../platform/verification.md). Provincial licensing body names are not stated on the dentist pages. [NEEDS CONFIRMATION]
