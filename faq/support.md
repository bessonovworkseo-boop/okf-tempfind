# Tempfind support and contact FAQ

## How do I contact Tempfind?
Use the contact form at https://tempfind.com/contact-us/ or email info@tempfind.com. The site shows two phone numbers, 1-877-455-3513 and 416-839-6682; the site does not say which is the primary support line.[^home][^cancellation]

## Where is the Help Center?
At https://tempfind.com/help-center/, with sections for clinics and for professionals. See the [Help Center list](../citations/help-center.md).

## What support do plans include?
All clinic plans include email and chat support. Business and Enterprise get priority support, and Enterprise adds dedicated account management.[^pricing]

## Is there a mobile app?
Yes, on the Apple App Store and Google Play.[^home]
