# Tempfind clinic plans and fees

Dental clinics pay Tempfind either per completed shift or through a monthly membership. Dental professionals pay nothing. Every shift paid through the platform also carries a 3.9% payment handling fee.[^pricing][^payment][^placement-fee]

## Which plans are available?

| Plan | Price | Placement fee | Term | Locations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pay Per Shift (also called Pay-Per-Hire) | $49 per completed shift | $49 per shift | No subscription | One location |
| Business | $149 per month per location | $0 | 12-month minimum | One location per membership |
| Enterprise | Custom | $0 | Per signed agreement | Multi-location practices and DSOs |
| Essential (grandfathered) | $99 per month | $0 | Kept only while the subscription stays active | Closed to new clinics since June 30, 2025 |

All prices are shown in dollars without a named currency; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).

## What does each plan include?

- **Pay Per Shift:** post jobs and accept candidates, request temps directly, chat with professionals, save favourite temps. No credit card needed to start.
- **Business:** everything in Pay Per Shift plus unlimited temp shift hires, HR Concierge for long-term hires, a dedicated clinic success manager and priority support. Instant shift booking is listed as coming soon.
- **Enterprise:** everything in Business plus volume-based discounts, shift payout and billing customization, a centralized admin dashboard and dedicated account management. Monthly performance reports are listed as coming soon.

All plans include email and chat support.

## Which plan fits which clinic?

The pricing page suggests a monthly plan when a clinic expects roughly 20 or more temporary hires per year. Pay Per Shift fits occasional or seasonal needs.

## What other fees apply?

- Payment handling fee: 3.9% of each shift total. Example: a $300 shift carries about $11.70. See [Payments and payouts](../policies/payment-policy.md).
- Business plan early cancellation: $298 in months 1 to 10, $149 in month 11, none from month 12.
- Non-circumvention and direct-hire fees. See [Non-circumvention rules](../policies/non-circumvention-policy.md).

## How do renewals and plan changes work?

- Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 30 days before renewal. Reminders go out at least 7 days ahead.
- Downgrades take effect immediately with no prorated refund.
- Re-upgrading to Business restarts the 12-month commitment.
- Clinics switch plans in the Billing section.
- Tempfind gives at least 30 days' notice of price changes.

## Is there a free option for clinics?

Pay Per Shift has no subscription fee and needs no card to start; fees apply per completed shift. Some pages mention a free trial and state that the job board route has no fee. The pricing page lists $49 per completed shift for Pay Per Shift; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).
