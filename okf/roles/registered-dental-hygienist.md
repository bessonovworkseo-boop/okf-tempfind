# Registered dental hygienist (RDH) on Tempfind

Dental clinics use Tempfind to book registered dental hygienists for temporary shifts, and hygienists use it to find temp hygiene work. A hygienist must submit a valid licence number before the profile goes live. Hygienists set their own hourly rate, which clinics see before booking.

## How does a clinic book a temporary hygienist?

1. Register the clinic and complete the profile.
2. Post a hygiene shift with date, start time and duration, or search hygienist profiles and send a booking request.
3. Review profiles: education, work history, software experience, languages, certifications, ratings and completed shifts.
4. Confirm the booking.

Most hygiene shifts on the platform run 6 to 8 hours. Shorter and longer bookings are possible.

## What do hygienist profiles show?

- Verified badges for government ID, professional licence, police check (when applicable) and certification documents.
- Additional certifications such as HARP, restorative dentistry or orthodontics, where the hygienist holds them.
- Hourly rate, star rating and number of completed shifts.

## How much do temporary hygienists charge?

Hygienists set their own rates. Sample profiles on the hiring page show rates from $50 to $63 per hour. These are examples, not platform rates. Clinic fees are separate; see [Clinic plans and fees](../pricing/clinic-plans.md).

## How does a hygienist find shifts?

Create a free profile, pass verification, set availability and rate, then apply to hygiene shifts or accept booking requests. See [Services for professionals](../platform/for-professionals.md).
