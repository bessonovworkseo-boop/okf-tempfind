# Dental floater on Tempfind

A dental floater is a cross-trained team member who covers more than one role in a clinic. Clinics use Tempfind to book floaters for flexible temporary coverage, and floaters use it to find temp shifts. Booking, verification, payment and cancellation follow the same platform rules as other roles.

## How does a clinic book a floater?

Post a shift listing the roles to cover, or search floater profiles and send a booking request. See [How Tempfind works](../platform/how-it-works.md).

## How does a floater find shifts?

Create a free profile, pass verification, set availability and hourly rate, then apply or accept booking requests. See [Services for professionals](../platform/for-professionals.md).
