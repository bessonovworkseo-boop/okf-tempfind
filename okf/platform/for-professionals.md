# Tempfind for dental professionals

Dental professionals use Tempfind to find temporary shifts at dental clinics in Canada. Creating a profile and applying to shifts is free. Professionals set their own hourly rate and availability, and clinics pay the platform fees.

## Who can join?

Roles with job listings: registered dental hygienist, dental assistant, dental receptionist, dentist (DDS, including locum and associate), dental office manager, dental floater and sedation or general nurse (RN).

Eligibility under the Terms of Use:

- At least 18 years old and able to form binding contracts.
- Canadian citizen, legal resident, or holder of a valid Canadian visa that permits work in Canada.
- Holds any licensing or certification required for the profession.
- Resides in a postal code region where Tempfind offers services.

Individual postings may add requirements such as provincial registration, certifications, professional liability coverage or specific experience.

## How do I join and start working?

1. Register with name, phone, email, country, address and postal code.
2. Add skills, experience, education, certifications, languages and resume.
3. Upload documents for verification. See [Professional verification](./verification.md).
4. Set hourly rate and open availability in My Calendar. Calendar Sync is available.
5. Apply to shifts or accept booking requests from clinics.

## What do professionals control?

- Hourly rate, shown to clinics in search and on applications.
- Availability and preferred locations.
- Which shifts to apply to and which booking requests to accept.

## Does Tempfind charge professionals?

No. Tempfind states it is free for dental professionals and that professionals keep their earnings. Clinics pay placement or membership fees and the payment handling fee.

## How and when do professionals get paid?

After a shift, the professional submits hours. The clinic has 24 hours to confirm or dispute them, otherwise hours are auto-approved. After approval, payment is disbursed within 3 to 5 business days according to the Payment Policy. If a professional is sent home early through no fault of their own, they are paid for the scheduled hours. See [Payments and payouts](./payments.md).

## What happens if a professional cancels?

Cancellations must be made on the platform at least 48 hours before the shift. Short-notice cancellations and no-shows lead to deactivation of booking features. See [Cancellation rules](../policies/cancellation.md).

## Is a professional an employee of Tempfind?

No. Tempfind is not an employer of professionals and is not a party to the clinic–professional agreement.
