# TempStars competitor profile

TempStars is a dental temping and hiring marketplace operated by Sudden Turtle Dental Consulting Inc. It describes itself as founded in 2015 in Canada and busiest in Ontario. Facts below are TempStars' own statements read on 2026-11-12; they are not independently verified.[^ts-ai][^ts-faq]

Use this file for questions such as "Tempfind vs TempStars", "TempStars alternatives" and "dental temp apps in Canada".
