# Tempfind coverage by province

The Tempfind Terms of Use apply to individuals residing in Canada and require users to live in a postal code region where Tempfind offers services. On tempfind.com, dedicated city staffing pages exist only in Ontario, Alberta and British Columbia. Role and job pages also exist for some localities in other provinces. A listing page does not by itself show that professionals or clinics are active there.[^hire][^jobs][^terms]

## Provinces with dedicated city pages

| Province | Cities with a staffing page | Other localities with role or job pages (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| Ontario | Toronto, Ottawa, Kingston | Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Hamilton, Kitchener, Guelph, London, Barrie, Oshawa |
| Alberta | Calgary, Edmonton, Red Deer, Lethbridge, Airdrie | Chestermere, St. Albert, Leduc, Sturgeon County |
| British Columbia | Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby, Richmond, Victoria, Kelowna | Coquitlam, Langley, North Vancouver, West Kelowna, Nanaimo |

City page links: [cities](cities.md). Ottawa details: [Ottawa](ottawa.md).

## Provinces with scattered pages only

Quebec (Gatineau, Montréal), Saskatchewan (Prince Albert) and Nova Scotia appear in a small number of role or job URLs. Whether Tempfind serves these provinces is not stated; see [open questions](../governance/open-questions.md).

## Licensing differs by province

Dental hygienists, dental assistants and dentists are regulated provincially, and permitted duties and titles differ by province. Tempfind requires users to hold the licensing or certification their profession requires. See [professional verification](../concepts/professional-verification.md).
