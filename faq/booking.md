# Booking a dental temp on Tempfind FAQ

## How does a clinic book a temporary dental professional?
Two ways. Post a shift and accept one of the applicants, or search the professional pool and send a booking request; the first professional to accept gets the shift. See [how it works](../guides/how-it-works.md).[^clinics]

## Which roles can a clinic book?
Registered dental hygienists, dental assistants, dental receptionists, dentists (DDS, locum and associate), dental office managers and dental floaters. Sedation or general nurse (RN) booking status is unconfirmed. See [concepts](../concepts/index.md).[^clinics]

## How long is a typical shift?
Role pages state that most shifts run 6 to 8 hours. Clinics set the date, start time and duration, and shorter or longer bookings are possible.[^rdh]

## Is there a minimum number of shifts?
No. A clinic can post a single shift or manage ongoing coverage.[^ottawa]

## Can a clinic chat with a professional before booking?
Yes. Tempfind has built-in chat, and the Help Center describes contacting a professional before accepting or booking them.[^clinics]

## Can a clinic book the same professional again?
Yes. Clinics can save professionals as favourites and send direct booking requests. Booking a Tempfind-connected professional outside the platform within 12 months breaches the [non-circumvention policy](../policies/non-circumvention-policy.md).
