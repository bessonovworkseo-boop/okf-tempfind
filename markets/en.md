# Tempfind English knowledge reference

Tempfind is an online platform, operated by Tempfind Inc. in Ontario, that connects dental clinics in Canada with temporary dental professionals. This page gives the short, sourced answer to the questions people ask most, and links to the record that holds the detail. Current policies on tempfind.com take precedence.[^home][^terms]

## Core facts

| Question | Answer | Record |
|---|---|---|
| What is it? | A dental staffing platform, not an agency or employer | [company](../brand/company.md), [platform vs agency](../concepts/staffing-platform-vs-agency.md) |
| Who uses it? | Dental clinics and DSOs; hygienists, assistants, receptionists, dentists, office managers, floaters | [products](../products/index.md), [roles](../concepts/index.md) |
| How do clinics book? | Post a shift and accept an applicant, or send booking requests; first to accept gets the shift | [how it works](../guides/how-it-works.md) |
| What does it cost clinics? | $49 per completed shift, or $149 per month per location (12-month term), or Enterprise; plus 3.9% handling fee | [pricing](../products/pricing.md) |
| What does it cost professionals? | Nothing | [for professionals](../products/for-professionals.md) |
| How are shifts paid? | By card through Tempfind; payout 3 to 5 business days after hours are approved | [payment policy](../policies/payment-policy.md) |
| Are professionals verified? | Yes, manual document review by staff before they can work | [verification](../concepts/professional-verification.md) |
