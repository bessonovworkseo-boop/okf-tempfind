# Tempfind professional verification FAQ

## Are professionals on Tempfind verified?
Yes. After registration, Tempfind staff manually review each professional's documents. Until a professional is verified, they cannot apply to jobs or be booked.[^verification]

## What is checked?
Credentials, certifications and education with supporting documents, document authenticity, and identity and security checks where the profession requires them. Role pages state that hygienists must submit a valid licence number and that verified badges cover government ID, professional licence, police check when applicable, and certification documents.[^verification][^rdh]

## Is verification automated?
No. The Help Center states that verification is controlled by Tempfind employees and that the system makes no automatic decisions.[^verification]

## How long does verification take?
Tempfind does not publish a timeline.

## How do clinics judge quality after a shift?
Clinics rate professionals after each completed shift, and profiles show star ratings and completed-shift counts. Professionals can also rate clinics.[^rdh]

## Who must hold a licence?
Every user must hold the licensing or certification their profession requires, under the Terms of Use.[^terms]
